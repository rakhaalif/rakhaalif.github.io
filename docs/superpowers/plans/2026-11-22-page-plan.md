# Page plan: the React rebuild of rakhaalif.github.io

Kind: software (page type software, site-kind software). A QA and AI engineer's portfolio, read by recruiters; the one main action is Download CV.

Deal: hero collage, palette candy-on-black, type chivo (Chivo and Chivo Mono), radius soft, nav nav-centre, footer footer-statement, close close-word, rhythm surface-pairs, buttons solid, mark initial, lit ground sky, effect fluted-glass in the hero frame, grounds bg-mist on data-projects and bg-band on certificates, moments pinned-chapters on projects and liquid-type on the close's name. The palette fits an AI engineer's page: a black ground reads as a terminal and an editor, and the teal accent is the colour of a passing test line.

The palette changed. The old site ran green-black-bone (ground #161c1a, ink #e3e2da, accent #9fc7b8, the owner's earlier pick); the deal gave candy-on-black, and this build uses the dealt one. The dealt tokens are below and bind the build (ruling R5).

## Voice card

- Voice: technical-dry (copy-registers.md 2), first person, as on the old site.
- Pairs: exact and plain; sure of the facts, no boasts.
- Person: "I" for Rakha's work, "the agent" for the tool; the demo speaks in the agent's terms.
- Sentences: 8 to 20 words; one long one a section where it explains.
- Humour: none.
- House words: test cases, functional, visual, run, agent, LLM, RAG, the same word every time.
- Never: virtue words, a pun on the name, invented figures, "live AI" or anything that makes the replay read as a real run.

## Rules every section keeps

- Every section is `main > section.section` with one `.wrap`, its h2 first, `--h: 1` or `2` (layout-v4.md section 2). Split pair: the 5/7 split (text on span-5, the visual on span-6 start-7, x 732), used on every split.
- Buttons are `.btn.btn--primary` and `.btn.btn--secondary` only, `data-buttons="solid"`; the primary carries `svg.m-arrow` in the hero and the close. Download CV is the one filled button, word for word in the nav, the hero, the close and `nav.actionbar`.
- `<html lang="en" data-type="software" data-radius="soft" data-buttons="solid" data-ground="sky" data-motion>`, `meta site-kind` and `meta page-type` both `software`. Title verbatim (ruling R3): Rakha Alif Athallah: QA and AI engineer, Universitas Brawijaya. Theme-color #000000.
- Keep the `:root` block above exactly as printed, and the Google Fonts link for Chivo and Chivo Mono with `display=swap`.
- The body shell is `header.nav`, `main#top`, `footer.closing`, `nav.actionbar` (plus scripts and the bumper). Every block in `main` is a `section` (the hero included), and nothing sits in `main` outside one [one-section]. The header, each section and the footer hold one in-flow `.wrap` on the same lines (x 80 to 1360 at 1440, 24 to 366 at 390) [container-edges, container].
- The hero is exactly the first screen: `100svh`, `--h: 1; --hm: 1`, its content ending by y 836 at 1440x900, nothing of `#agent` showing at 1440, 1024, 820, 768 or 390 [hero-screen]. The first screen's hard fact is the GPA 3.80 in the lead [copy-fact]. Download CV is a button in the hero and in the close [cta].
- The two moments' hosts (`#projects[data-moment]`, `.close__word[data-moment]`) are in the DOM before the kit's `moments.js` loader starts, so start it after React has mounted the page [moment-none].
- Scroll motion lives only in the two dealt moments, plus the experience line the spec asks for (see Motion). No section, card or row fades in on scroll: the kit's Never list forbids it and the checker FAILs `scroll-reveal`, so Task 7's `useReveal` hook is dropped (ruling R4).
